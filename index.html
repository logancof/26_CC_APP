BAPTISM & TESTIMONY SERVICE DETAILS

6:30-8:00 p.m.Evening Service
8:00-8:15 p.m.Dismissal from Evening Service to Lake
8:15 p.m.Begin Baptism & Testimony Service
8:20 p.m.Student Testimonies
8:35 p.m.Baptisms
9:15 p.m.Service Ending

Dismissal from Evening Service (Auditorium) to Lake

  • At the conclusion of Evening Service, our hosts will begin dismissing students to head to the lake, beginning with students who are signed up to be baptized.
  • Next Gen Coordinators are responsible for connecting with the students in their campus outside the auditorium, walking them down to the lake, and lining them up alphabetically. A list of students who are signed up in each campus will be provided to the respective Next Gen Coordinator.
  • Each campus will line up at its designated labeled post.
  • After approximately 5 minutes, our hosts will begin dismissing the rest of the students by grade. The remaining students will walk to the lake with their CC Leader, who will have their lanterns for lighting.

Baptism & Testimony Service

  • After Carson opens the service, selected students will share their testimonies while the band is playing in the background.
  • Following student testimonies, we will begin baptisms. Students will be baptized by campus in the order of campus launch (Lewisburg-Greenville). Students, volunteers, and visiting families will be invited to join in on our acoustic worship.

Service Closing

  • Carson will dismiss students for their breakout sessions. All students need to stay with their CC Leader through breakout sessions.
  • After students are dismissed, visitors will be dismissed. Our Safety and Office Crew will ensure a smooth check-out process for our visiting families.

Details for Visitors

  • Parents, guardians, and families of students being baptized are invited to join us for our Baptism & Testimony Service.
  • Visitors are asked to arrive between 7:00-7:30 p.m. They will park with the guidance of our Safety Crew, and they will check in under the shelter by the lake with our Office Crew, receiving a visitor wristband. Refreshments will be served as they await the arrival of the students.
  • Community Camp Leaders will personally invite the families of those who are being baptized, including a visitor registration link.
  • Visitors must fill out the following form ahead of time: https://cofchurches.com/cc-visitor
  • Following the service, visitors will be asked to leave together, as their cars will be lined up back-to-back. As they exit, all visitors will check out with the Office Crew from their vehicles and return their visitor wristbands.
